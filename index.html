BEHIND THE IMAGE.
INSIDE THE STORY.

Pranav Chandane is a cinematographer, director and creative producer specialising in feature films, web series, advertising campaigns and large-scale live events.

His work spans BTS cinematography, still photography, Electronic Press Kits (EPKs), marketing films and digital campaign content — transforming the filmmaking process into compelling visual stories.

He works across complex productions involving multiple teams, cameras and fast-paced shooting environments, from intimate creative moments and interviews to large-scale action sequences and live events.

THE
PEASANT

A DEFINING CHAPTER.

A defining project in my journey as a filmmaker. A feature film produced by A24, The Peasant gave me the opportunity to work closely within an ambitious international production, documenting the filmmaking process from the ground up.

I served as the BTS Cinematographer, while also leading the creative direction and execution of the EPK and behind-the-scenes content. My work involved capturing filmmaker and cast interviews, documenting the craft and scale of the production, and filming large-scale action sequences.

The aim was to create cinematic BTS and EPK films that went beyond documentation — bringing the film’s vision, craftsmanship, performances and filmmaking process to life for an international audience.
